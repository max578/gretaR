# Constellation membership — gretaR

> **This project is a MEMBER of the flexyBayes Constellation.** Joined 2026-06-02.
> A constellation is a set of independent R&D projects organised in full support
> of one leader. This file is gretaR's back-pointer to that leader and charter.

- **Leader:** flexyBayes.
- **My role:** engine — torch-native Bayesian MCMC (no Python); a producer.
- **Contracts I honour:** C1 `backend_contract` (v1, owned by flexyBayes).
- **My edge:** gretaR -> flexyBayes — status *activating* (ratified 2026-06-02);
  non-hierarchical live, default hierarchical fitting gated on the NUTS perf fix.
- **What binds me (charter invariants):**
  - *Standalone-functional* — gretaR must keep working and `R CMD check` clean
    with no sibling member installed; no hard `Imports:` on another member.
  - *Version floor* — the C1 edge requires gretaR >= 0.4.0 (currently met).
  - *Leader-directed adoption* — I align my R&D to the leader's published
    direction; I may propose innovations (a `constellation_c1` ledger cairn), the
    leader arbitrates adoption by the prospects of leader + team.
- **Canonical charter:** `flexyBayes_dev/CONSTELLATION.md` (also in the MaxAIbase
  brain, open tier). Leader decision: cairn
  `flexyBayes_dev/ledger/infrastructure/2026-06-02-constellation-charter-v01`.

I remain independently developed and separately publishable. Membership is
cooperation, not coupling.
